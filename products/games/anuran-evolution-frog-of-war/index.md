# Anuran Evolution: Frog of War

<span style={{ color: '#00A9FF', fontWeight: 'bold', fontSize: '1.2em' }}>A thrilling multiplayer first-person shooter set in a world of amphibious warfare.</span>

Built with [Unreal Engine 5](https://www.unrealengine.com/en-US/unreal-engine-5), Anuran Evolution: Frog of War drops you into the battlefield as an agile, fearless frog. Fight for survival across multiple game modes — from intense deathmatches to adrenaline-pumping battle royale — all in a uniquely fun and competitive frog-filled universe. Wishlist Now!
